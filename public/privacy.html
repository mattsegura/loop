Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

Loop (useloop.sh) is a hosted gateway that lets your AI agents share the connections, skills, memory and keys you set up once. This policy explains what Loop stores, why, and what control you have.

What we collect

  • Account details. When you sign up we receive your email address, name and profile picture from our sign-in provider (Clerk, or Google if you choose "Continue with Google"). We use them only to sign you in and identify your workspace.
  • Workspace data you add. Connections (MCP server addresses and settings), saved Vault items such as API keys and tokens, skills, memory items, agent names and agent keys.
  • Activity records. A log of which agent used which tool, resource or prompt and when. Tool arguments and results are not stored in this log.
  • Feedback about Loop, only if you allow it. When one of your agents meets a fault of Loop itself, it asks you if it may send us a short report. Without your yes it sends nothing, unless you ask it to tell us something. Reports are about Loop and not about your work: we tell agents to leave your data out, and we remove email addresses, phone and account numbers, links and credentials. We do not keep your name or email address with a report. You can see and delete each report, and stop this at any time, in Settings → Security. Your agents can also stop it for you.
  • Technical data. IP addresses and request metadata used for security, abuse prevention and rate limiting, kept in short-lived server logs.

How we use it

Only to run Loop for you: authenticate you and your agents, forward your agents' requests to the connections you configured, show you your activity, keep the service secure, and contact you about your account. We use the feedback that you allow to fix and improve Loop. We do not sell your data, use it for advertising, or use your workspace content to train AI models.

Google user data

If you sign in with Google, Loop requests only your basic profile and email address (the openid, email and profile scopes). This information is used solely to create and sign in to your Loop account. Loop's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We do not transfer it to others except as needed to provide sign-in (Clerk), and we never use it for advertising.

How your data is protected

All traffic uses HTTPS. Vault values are encrypted at rest and are never shown to agents or included in activity logs. Each workspace is isolated to the signed-in account that owns it, and agents need their own workspace key.

Where it is stored and who processes it

  • Hetzner Online GmbH (Nuremberg, Germany): servers, storage and backups.
  • Clerk, Inc. (United States): account sign-in and session management.
  • Google: only if you choose Google sign-in.
  • Anthropic PBC (United States): sorts feedback about Loop, only if you allow it.

When your agents call a connection, Loop sends that request to the third-party service you configured. That service's own privacy policy applies to what it receives.

Retention and deletion

Workspace data is kept until you delete it or close your account. Activity logs are automatically trimmed. Feedback about Loop is kept for up to 180 days. Backups are kept for up to 14 days. To delete your account and all workspace data, email the address below and we will complete the request within 30 days.

Your rights

You can access, correct, export or delete your data, and object to or restrict its processing, by contacting us. If you are in the EU/EEA or UK you may also complain to your local data protection authority.

Changes

We will post any changes here and update the date above. Material changes will be announced in the app or by email.
